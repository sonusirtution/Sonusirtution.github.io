#01 ELIGIBILITY

Who Can Apply (Session 2027–28)

  • Eligible Classes: Students entering Class 5, 6, 7, 8, 9, or 10th for the 2027–28 academic session.
  • Supported Boards: Exclusively for ICSE and CBSE board curriculums (English Medium).
  • Subject Package: Focused coaching in Mathematics, Physics, Chemistry, and Biology.
#02 PRIOR NOTICE & DISCIPLINE

Attendance & Prior Information Policy

  • Prior Information Required for Absence: Regular classroom attendance is essential. If a student has to miss a class due to illness or an urgent family reason, prior information from a parent/guardian via phone call or WhatsApp to Sonu Sir is mandatory.
  • Punctuality: Students must report 5 minutes before their allotted slot (2:45 PM or 4:15 PM).
  • Separate Notebooks: Dedicated classwork & homework notebooks for Maths, Physics, Chemistry & Biology are compulsory.
#03 EVALUATION

Homework & Chapter-Test Terms

  • Daily Practice: Assigned numerical problems, derivations, and diagrams must be completed before the next class.
  • Mandatory Chapter Tests: No student is exempted from weekly/bi-weekly ICSE & CBSE pattern written assessments.
  • Parent Feedback: Test marks and notebook remarks are shared directly with parents/guardians.
#04 ADMISSION

Parent Meeting & Batch Allocation

  • Compulsory Parent Meeting: Admission is finalized only after an in-person discussion between the parent, student, and Sonu Sir.
  • Limited Batch Seats: To preserve 1-on-1 attention, each afternoon batch has a strict seat cap.
  • Fee & Schedule Discipline: Monthly tuition fees are payable within the first 10 days of each month.
